The source the whole lab uses: eight seconds of test pattern,
generated by ffmpeg's testsrc2 -- drawn, not filmed, and nobody's
property. Two tones stand in for two audio tracks, an invented
subtitle file stands in for dialogue, and two chapter markers
split the eight seconds in half.

$ cat subs.srt
1
00:00:00,500 --> 00:00:03,500
A test pattern, drawn by a program.

2
00:00:04,500 --> 00:00:07,500
No camera was involved in this video.

$ ffmpeg -hide_banner -y \
  -f lavfi -i testsrc2=size=1280x720:rate=25:duration=8 \
  -f lavfi -i sine=frequency=440:duration=8 \
  -f lavfi -i "sine=frequency=880:duration=8,pan=stereo|c0=c0|c1=c0" \
  -i subs.srt -i chapters.ffmeta \
  -map 0:v -map 1:a -map 2:a -map 3:s \
  -map_metadata 4 -map_chapters 4 \
  -c:v libx264 -preset medium -crf 16 -pix_fmt yuv420p \
  -c:a aac -b:a 96k -c:s srt \
  -metadata:s:a:0 title="Tone A - 440 Hz mono" \
  -metadata:s:a:1 title="Tone B - 880 Hz stereo" \
  testcard.mkv

$ ls -la testcard.mkv
-rw-rw---- 1 root root 4810247 testcard.mkv
